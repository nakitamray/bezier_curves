Bézier Curves

A Bézier curve is a weighted average of its control points. Drag a point and watch which parts of the equations it touches.

Bernstein form

Each control point gets a weight from a Bernstein polynomial. The weights always add up to 1, so B(t) is an average of the points.

Matrix form

Expanding the Bernstein polynomials into powers of t gives the same curve as a product of three matrices.

Tangent

The derivative is another Bézier curve, one degree lower, built from the differences between neighbouring points.
