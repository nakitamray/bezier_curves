Bernstein form

B(t) is a weighted average of the points. Hover a row or a point to see where it shows up.

Matrix form

The numbers in P are the points. Drag one sideways to move that point.

How to play with the curve

  • drag a pointreshape the curve
  • drag the yellow dotslide along the curve (that is t)
  • shift + clickadd a control point there
  • double-tap a pointremove it
  • drag / scrollmove around and zoom the board
  • double-tap emptyreset the view
  • drag 2.50numbers with a dotted yellow line under them are handles. drag one left or right to change it (shift = fine)
  • spaceplay / pause t
  • cardsdrag one by its title, – makes it smaller, "hide cards" clears the board

About

Locus is an interactive math playground made by Nakita Ray. nakitaray.com

Built with

HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript on the Canvas 2D API. No frameworks or build step: the 3D renderer, plotting and expression parser are written from scratch. Equations are typeset with KaTeX.
