The equation

At every point (x, y) the equation gives a slope. The little line segments are those slopes, and a solution is a curve that stays tangent to all of them.

Euler's method

Take a step of size h in the direction the slope points, then look again. Smaller steps follow the true curve (white) more closely.

Eigenvalues decide everything

Solutions along an eigenvector stay on that line and grow or shrink like eλt. Complex eigenvalues make the solutions spin.

The system

Nullclines: x′ = 0 is drawn in cream, y′ = 0 in yellow. Equilibria sit where they cross.

Linearizing at each equilibrium

Near an equilibrium the system acts like x′ = Jx, where J is the Jacobian. Its eigenvalues give the local picture, just like the linear case.
